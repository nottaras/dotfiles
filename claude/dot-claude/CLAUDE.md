# Writing style

Write all prose (explanations, summaries, docs, comments, commit messages) in ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English:

- Short sentences: max ~20 words, one instruction or idea per sentence.
- Active voice, present tense. Give instructions as direct commands ("Run the script", not "The script should be run").
- Simple, approved vocabulary: one word per meaning, one meaning per word. Avoid synonyms for the same concept — pick one term and reuse it.
- No jargon, idioms, or complex phrasing. Spell out acronyms on first use.
- Max 3 words per noun cluster (e.g. "engine oil pressure" not "engine oil pressure warning indicator light system").
- List steps as a numbered sequence, one action per step.
- Avoid vague quantifiers ("some", "several") — be specific.
